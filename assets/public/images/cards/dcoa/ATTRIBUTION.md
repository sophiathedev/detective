# Detective: City of Angels case-card assets

Publisher: Van Ryder Games

Artist credit: Vincent Dutrait

Publisher page: https://vanrydergames.com/products/detective-city-of-angels

Official Case Design Kit: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Pv9FxFB6w_93m9mgF3ZQ63KPVORO3X_w/view

Van Ryder Games publishes the Case Design Kit for people creating fan-made cases. This project uses the kit's A-L card backs, blank front templates, and three generic illustrations for its original sample case.

The runtime WebP files are responsive sharp-YUV derivatives of the official PNG sources. Browsers choose between compact 220px, standard 420px, and 675px zoom variants according to the rendered card width and board zoom. This keeps the normal board lightweight while preserving detail during deliberate zoom. They do not contain the secret text or solutions from any commercial case.

This asset use is limited to this public, non-commercial fan project. The project is unofficial and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Van Ryder Games. Obtain permission or replace these assets before any commercial use.
